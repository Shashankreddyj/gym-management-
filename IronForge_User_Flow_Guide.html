IronForge Gym OS — User Flow Guide

AI-Native Gym Management System · 22 Modules · August 2026

How To Read This Guide: Each module shows the step-by-step flow (➔) and the screens/components involved. All pages are accessible from the sidebar navigation. AI-powered modules are marked with 🤖.

1. Dashboard

Login / Open App ➔ Dashboard loads automatically

What you see: KPI cards (members, revenue, occupancy), attendance chart with AI forecast, churn risk bar chart, 3 live sections below (Churn Alerts, Lead Response, Class Optimization).

Step-by-Step:

1 App opens → Dashboard is the default home page.
2 Review AI Action Required banner — click "Review Now" to jump to Retention page.
3 Toggle timeframe (Day / Week / Month) to filter KPI data view.
4 Click any churn alert card → navigates to that member's profile.
5 Click any lead card → navigates to Leads page.
6 Click any class → navigates to Schedule page.

2. Members (Core Operations)

Dashboard ➔ Click Members in sidebar

Step-by-Step:

1 Navigate to Members page → see full table with search, filter, churn risk bars.
2 Click "Add Member" → modal opens → enter name, email, phone, select plan → submit.
3 Member appears instantly in the list with success toast.
4 Use filter tabs (All / Active / At-Risk / PastDue) to segment members.
5 Use search bar to find by name or email.
6 Click any member row → navigates to Member Profile page.

3. Member Profile

Members List ➔ Click member row ➔ Member Profile

Step-by-Step:

1 View member header (name, plan, status, contact buttons).
2 See 360° score cards: Engagement, Churn Risk, Streak, Lifetime Value.
3 View engagement trend chart (8-week history).
4 For at-risk members: click "Trigger AI Outreach" → sends WhatsApp + Email.
5 Click AI recommendation actions (Send Offer / Take Action / Recommend).
6 Review Quick Stats, Goals, and Communication Log sections.
7 Click "Back to Members" to return.

4. Schedule (Core Operations)

Sidebar ➔ Click Schedule

Step-by-Step:

1 View class cards with fill rate %, time, trainer, room.
2 Click "Add Class" → modal → fill class name, trainer, time, type, room, capacity → submit.
3 New class card appears immediately.
4 Click day selector (Today / Mon / Tue…) to filter.
5 Read AI recommendations on underbooked classes (highlighted cards).

5. Billing & Revenue

Sidebar ➔ Click Billing

Step-by-Step:

1 View revenue KPIs (Monthly Revenue, Collection Rate, Pending, Failed).
2 Review Revenue vs Expenses chart with AI forecast overlay.
3 Click "Export Report" → downloads CSV file to your computer.
4 Click AI insight action buttons → shows confirmation toast.
5 Filter transactions by Paid / Pending / Failed using tabs.

6. Check-In

Sidebar ➔ Click Check-In

Step-by-Step:

1 Click "Show QR" to display QR code (member scans at kiosk).
2 Click "Simulate Scan" → 2-second scanning animation → success card shows member name + time.
3 View live check-in counter, peak hours, currently inside count.
4 Click AI insight actions (View Member, Security Log, Manage Kiosks).

7. POS / Inventory

Sidebar ➔ Click POS / Inventory

Step-by-Step:

1 Filter products by category (All / Supplements / Merchandise / Accessories / Beverages / Snacks).
2 Click a product card → adds to cart (click again to increase qty).
3 In cart: adjust quantity (+/-), remove items (trash icon).
4 Click "Checkout" → success animation with total.
5 View Today's Sales summary and Low Stock Alerts below.

8. Staff & Trainers

Sidebar ➔ Click Staff & Trainers

Step-by-Step:

1 View all trainers as cards (name, role, rating, utilization, commission).
2 Click a trainer card → expands to show "Approve Commission" button.
3 Click "Approve Commission" → shows confirmed status.
4 Review Schedule Conflicts section with severity badges (High / Low).
5 Click "Resolve All" to clear conflicts.

9. Equipment Heatmap

Sidebar ➔ Core Operations ➔ Equipment Heatmap

Step-by-Step:

1 See live gym occupancy % and "Best Time Today" recommendation.
2 View Hourly Zone Utilization chart (Cardio / Weights / Strength / Functional).
3 Read Zone Status cards showing occupancy bars + best time to visit.
4 Filter equipment by zone (All / Cardio / Free Weights / Strength / Functional).
5 Search for specific equipment by name.
6 Click equipment card → expands to show detailed stats (peak hour, wait time, trend).

10. 🤖 AI Lead Response

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ Leads

Step-by-Step:

1 View Kanban pipeline (New → Contacted → Trial Scheduled → Converted → Lost).
2 Click a lead card → selects/deselects it.
3 Click "Demo AI Call" → 4-step animation: AI detects lead → calls → connects → books trial in 23s.
4 Click "Add Lead" → modal → fill name, email, source, interest → submit.
5 New lead appears in "New" column. AI auto-responds in ~30 seconds.

11. 🤖 AI Retention Engine

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ Retention

Step-by-Step:

1 View Churn Signal Analysis chart (top reasons for churn).
2 Read "How It Works" section: Predict → Auto-Act → Staff Review → Measure.
3 Scroll to Active Churn Alerts — each shows member, risk level, reason, recommended action.
4 Click "Approve" → outreach simulation modal opens (4-step: approved → sent → member opens → recovered).
5 Click "Decline" → status changes to "Declined — member moved to watchlist."
6 Filter by All / Pending / Approved tabs.

12. 🤖 AI Front Desk

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ AI Front Desk

Step-by-Step:

1 View Active Conversations list (Voice / WhatsApp / SMS / Chat channels).
2 Click any conversation → chat window opens with member message + AI response.
3 Type in the input box → press Enter → AI responds after 800ms.
4 Click mic button → 2s voice animation → auto-fills text.
5 See channel icons showing which platform the conversation is on.

13. 🤖 Member 360°

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ Member 360°

Step-by-Step:

1 Click any member pill at top to switch members (8 available).
2 Radar chart updates showing 6 metrics (attendance, engagement, payments, classes, goals, social).
3 View AI Health Score ring (0-100) with breakdown items.
4 Click "View Full Profile" → navigates to Member Profile.
5 Scroll to Member Health Score Trend chart (all members, 8-month history).

14. 🤖 AI Business Intelligence

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ AI Business Intelligence

Step-by-Step:

1 Read AI Analyst Chat welcome message.
2 Click any Quick Query (Churn Risk / Revenue / Attendance / Leads / Classes / Members).
3 AI responds with tables, KPIs, or charts + insight paragraph.
4 Type custom question in the input → press Enter → AI responds with relevant analysis.
5 Type hints: "Show me revenue by plan" or "Which members are at risk?"

15. 🤖 No-Show Prediction

Sidebar ➔ AI & Automation ➔ No-Show Prediction

Step-by-Step:

1 View 5 member predictions with probability % bars and reasons.
2 See auto-reminded badges on members already contacted.
3 Click "Send Reminder Now" for manual outreach on un-reminded members.
4 View Smart Waitlist Backfill panel — click "Trigger Backfill" to auto-confirm waitlisted members.
5 Review deposit recommendation at bottom.
6 Click "Simulate AI Prevention" → 3-step modal: prediction → reminders → 62% prevention rate.

16. 🤖 Revenue Optimizer

Sidebar ➔ Growth & Scale ➔ Revenue Optimizer

Step-by-Step:

1 Toggle between Plan Upsells and Dynamic Pricing tabs.
2 View Upsell Pipeline chart (Ready → Warming → Nurturing).
3 For each upsell opportunity → click "Send Upgrade Offer" → shows confirmation.
4 For dynamic pricing → click "Apply Pricing" → shows "Dynamic pricing active" confirmation.
5 Review total revenue opportunity banner at top.

17. 🤖 Payment Recovery

Sidebar ➔ Growth & Scale ➔ Payment Recovery

Step-by-Step:

1 View recovery stats: Total Failed, Recovered, Recovery Rate, AI Outreach Success.
2 Read AI Dunning Sequence diagram (Day 1: Auto-Retry → Day 3: SMS → Day 7: Call → Day 14: Offer Plan).
3 For each dunning record → click "Mark Recovered" OR "Offer Plan".
4 View Active Payment Plans (installment offers accepted).
5 Click "Simulate Recovery" → 4-step modal showing full dunning workflow.

18. 🤖 AI Marketing Campaigns

Sidebar ➔ Growth & Scale ➔ AI Campaigns

Step-by-Step:

1 View 4 campaign cards (Win-Back, Referral, Class Boost, Upsell).
2 For Draft campaigns → click "Launch Campaign" → shows confirmation.
3 For Active campaigns → view open rates, conversion stats, revenue generated.
4 Scroll to A/B Test Results — see winning variants with improvement percentages.

19. Multi-Location

Sidebar ➔ Growth & Scale ➔ Multi-Location

Step-by-Step:

1 Click location pills (IronForge Main / West / South / East) to switch.
2 Per-location KPIs update for the selected location.
3 View Location Comparison bar chart (toggle Revenue / Members / Occupancy).
4 Review Performance Ranking list (sorted by revenue, shows growth %).

20. 🏋️ AI Workout Plan Generator

Sidebar ➔ Member Experience ➔ AI Workout Plan

Step-by-Step:

1 Enter your name (optional) in the input field.
2 Select a goal card: Weight Loss, Muscle Building, General Fitness, or Endurance.
3 Click "Generate My 4-Week Plan" → 2-second AI generation animation.
4 View full plan: red header with goal + description, 4 week tabs, 6 daily workout cards.
5 Click week tabs (W1–W4) → exercises update with progressive overload (more sets/reps).
6 Read Cardio Schedule and AI Coach Notes sections.
7 Click "Save Plan" or "New Plan" to start over.

21. 🏆 Leaderboard & Challenges

Sidebar ➔ Member Experience ➔ Leaderboard

Step-by-Step:

1 View 4 challenge cards at top — click any to select the active challenge.
2 See podium visualization (🥇🥈🥉) with top 3 members.
3 Scroll full leaderboard (10 members per challenge) with trend arrows.
4 Click "Join Challenge" → changes to "You're in! Good luck 🎉".
5 Browse Achievement Badges grid (10 badges).
6 View Your Stats (rank, challenges won, badges earned, best streak).

22. 🍎 Nutrition & Meal Plans

Sidebar ➔ Member Experience ➔ Nutrition & Meals

Step-by-Step:

1 View macro summary: Daily Calories, Protein Target, Meals, Pre/Post-Workout timing.
2 Tap water glasses (💧) to track intake — turns red when logged.
3 Select plan: Weight Loss (1800 cal) / Muscle Gain (2800 cal) / General Fitness (2200 cal).
4 View 6-meal daily schedule — click any meal to expand and see food list.
5 Read Recommended Supplements section (changes per selected goal).
6 Read AI Nutrition Tips at bottom.
7 Click "Save Plan" to confirm.

23. ⚙️ Settings

Sidebar ➔ Settings (bottom of sidebar)

Step-by-Step:

1 Edit Gym Profile fields (Name, Email, Phone, Location).
2 Toggle AI Automation settings (6 switches: churn outreach, lead response, voice receptionist, class fill, no-show predictions, dynamic pricing).
3 Review Integration status cards (Razorpay, Google Fit, Apple Health, Strava, etc.).
4 Click "Save Changes" → shows "Saved!" for 3 seconds.

24. 🌐 Global Features

Dark Mode Toggle

1 Click 🌙 moon icon in header → switches to dark theme.
2 Click ☀️ sun icon → switches back to light theme.
3 Preference saved in localStorage — persists across sessions.

Multi-Language (i18n)

1 Click 🇬🇧 globe icon in header → dropdown shows 5 languages.
2 Select any language (हिन्दी / தமிழ் / తెలుగు / मराठी) → sidebar labels update instantly.

Search

1 Use global search bar in header to search members, classes, leads.
